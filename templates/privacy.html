Policy

Your privacy matters. This page explains what data Spendly collects, how it is used, and the choices you have over your information.

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic account information such as your name, email address, and a hashed password. When you use the service, we store the expense records you choose to add, including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions or notes you provide. We also collect standard technical information such as IP address and browser type to keep the service secure and reliable.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data you provide to operate Spendly, present your expense history and category breakdowns, and improve the product over time. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. We may use aggregated, non-identifying statistics (for example, "users add an average of 3 expenses per day") to guide product decisions, but this data cannot be traced back to you.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers and transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Account passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms and are never stored in plain text. While no system is perfectly secure, we take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. You may delete your account at any time, in which case your personal data is permanently removed from active systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly relies on a small number of trusted third parties to operate, including a hosting provider, an email service for account notifications, and Google Fonts for typography. These providers receive only the minimum information needed to perform their function and are bound by their own privacy obligations. We do not embed advertising trackers or share your data with advertising networks.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, want to request a copy of your data, or would like to delete your account, please reach out to us at support@spendly.app. We aim to respond to all privacy-related requests within 7 business days. You can also review our Terms and Conditions for the full picture on how Spendly works.

Your data, your control

Spendly is built to help you — not to profit from your information.
